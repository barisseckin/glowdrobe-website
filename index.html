Questions

What is Glowdrobe?

Glowdrobe is an AI stylist and digital wardrobe app for iOS and Android. You add your clothes, tell it your plans, and it builds weather-aware outfits from what you own. You can also try a look on your own photo and plan your week on a calendar.

Is Glowdrobe free?

It's free to download. AI features such as outfit suggestions and virtual try-on use AI credits. You can buy a one-time pack of 10 AI credits or subscribe to Premium.

Do I need to buy new clothes?

No. Outfits are built from your closet, and shopping suggestions only appear when you ask for them.

How does the closet check work before I buy something?

Add a product from your browser, a screenshot or your photo library. Glowdrobe compares it with your closet and tells you if you already own something similar. The check itself uses no AI credits.

How does virtual try-on work?

Upload a photo of yourself, pick a piece or a whole outfit, and Glowdrobe generates a preview of the look on you. Results are saved in your try-on history.

How long does it take to set up my closet?

Minutes, not a weekend. Start with a starter closet, photograph your clothes, or import several items from one order screenshot. AI tags each piece for you.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are processed to deliver the feature you asked for, like tagging an item or generating a try-on. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I export my closet or delete my account?

Yes to both. You can back up and export your data, and you can delete your account right inside the app.

Is Glowdrobe on Android?

Yes, it's on the way. Glowdrobe is coming to both iOS and Android.

Which languages does Glowdrobe support?

The app is available in 14 languages: Arabic, Dutch, English, French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish and Turkish.
