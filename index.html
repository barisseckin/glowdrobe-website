FAQ

Good questions.

Is Glowdrobe free?

Yes. Glowdrobe is free to download. AI features such as outfit suggestions and virtual try-on use AI credits. You can buy a one-time pack of 10 AI credits or subscribe to Premium, which adds AI credits and extra wardrobe uploads every month. Prices are shown in the App Store.

Do I need to buy new clothes?

No. The whole point is to get more out of what you already own. The AI stylist builds outfits from the pieces in your digital wardrobe.

How does virtual try-on work?

Upload a photo of yourself, pick an outfit, and Glowdrobe generates a preview of the look on you. Your results are saved in your try-on history.

What happens to my photos?

Photos you upload are processed only to deliver the feature you asked for, like tagging an item or generating a try-on. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel Premium?

Premium is managed by Apple. Open your App Store account settings, go to Subscriptions and cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, right inside the app from your settings.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Glowdrobe is available for iPhone on the App Store, in English and Turkish.
